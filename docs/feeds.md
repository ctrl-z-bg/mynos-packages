# feeds

A reader of news feeds, RSS and Atom. The window lists the feeds of
`/disk/feeds.txt` (an address a line; at first: this project's commits,
OCaml's forum, Hacker News), their items, and the chosen item's text.
**Add** takes the address typed in the field, **Remove** the chosen
feed, **Reload** reads them all again.

```
mynos:/$ feeds https://discuss.ocaml.org/latest.rss     the items, in the terminal
```
