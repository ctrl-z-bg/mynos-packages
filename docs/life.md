# life

Conway's Game of Life, the example of chapter 20 of the manual grown up:
a board of 128 by 80 cells (its edges wrap), famous patterns to place
(a glider, a spaceship, the pulsar, the pentadecathlon, Gosper's glider
gun, the R-pentomino, the acorn, diehard), four speeds.

Click or drag on the board to draw. A pattern's button, then a click on
the board, places it (Escape: no). **Save** writes the board as
`/disk/life-N.rle`, in the RLE format of Life's collections (LifeWiki,
Golly); **Open** places a pattern read from such a file. Space runs and
pauses, S steps, R fills at random, C clears, Q quits.
