# mines

Find the mines of a field without stepping on one. A click opens a
square, which tells how many mines touch it; the right button marks a
mine (or the left one, with **Flags** on, for a touchpad). A click on an
open number whose mines are all marked opens the squares around it. The
first square you open is never a mine.

Three levels: Easy (9x9, 10 mines), Medium (16x16, 40), Hard (30x16, 99).
Keys: `1` `2` `3` the level, `N` a new game, `F` flags, `Q` quits.
