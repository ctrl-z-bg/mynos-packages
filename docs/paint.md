# paint

Drawing with the mouse: pencil, brush, line, box, block (filled), oval,
fill, eraser, and Pick (a colour from the picture); sixteen colours and
three sizes.

**Save** writes the picture as PNG, to the name in the field
(`/disk/picture.png` at first): compressed, readable anywhere. **Open**
reads PNG, BMP, PPM or PGM (cut to the canvas, or on white). **Undo**
(or U, or Ctrl-Z) takes back up to 16 strokes. Keys P B L R X O F E K
pick the tools, `[` and `]` the size, Q quits.
