# solitaire

Klondike patience. Build the four foundations up by suit, from the ace
to the king. On the seven columns, cards go down in alternate colours,
and only a king fills an empty column.

Drag cards with the mouse, or click one: it goes where it can, a
foundation first. Click the stock (top left) to turn a card; when it is
empty, a click turns the waste over. **Undo** (U) takes moves back;
**Auto** (A) sends to the foundations whatever can go; **New** (N) deals
again. Q quits.
